Voyage

How to play

  • ClickTap the water to set a course, or a place name to sail there.
  • Drag to look around the ship; scrollpinch to zoom.
  • Fire a broadside out the port (left) or starboard (right) side with Q / E. It reaches the nearest ship on that side.
  • Treasure is buried inland under a big red X. Open the map (M) to see them all; tap the map to set a course.
  • Drop anchor, then tap Ashore (B) to row the dinghy to the beach. Ashore, tap to walk, and Dig at an X. Your pistol fires straight ahead, ashore or from the dinghy. Only Back / Board gets you into the dinghy or aboard your ship.
  • A chest rides in your hands, then the dinghy, then your hold, and only counts once you bring it home to your hideout (H). Your hideout also repairs your hull.
  • Ashore you have 3 hearts: cannonballs landing nearby hurt. One hit sinks a dinghy.
  • Hold a straight course at full speed for 5 seconds and she makes 2 knots more.
  • Wind blows in patches (the blue arrow on the compass): run with it for up to 3 knots more, or fight it. Storms blow hardest.
  • Beware the Kraken, which grabs and squeezes ships, and the White Whale, which rams them. Cannon fire drives them off.
  • Sink Navy and merchant ships, then scoop up their floating coins. Navy frigates attack if they spot you ahead of their bow; sneak up from behind. Merchantmen fight back once attacked.
  • Ramming a ship's side with your bow hurts them far more than you; a sideswipe hurts both. Sink and your hold spills into the sea; you escape in the dinghy and row home for a fresh ship.
  • Anchor stops the ship (Space).

Tap the chart to set a course. X marks the spot!
